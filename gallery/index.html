Welcome, Curator!

Four rooms, 45 presidents, and someone mixed up the signs under the portraits! Check every one and seal each portrait under glass. Seal a whole room to open the door to the next one.

  • Drag (or use ⟲ ⟳) to look around the room.
  • Tap a portrait to walk up to it and check its signs.
  • Tap a gold circle on the floor to walk there.
  • Every room hides one museum treasure. Look for the sparkle!
