For grown-ups

What it teaches. Putting events in order, and seeing how one thing led to another. Every date and story on a real card was checked against a reliable source.

How cards are kept. A card goes in the binder when your child places it correctly. A card missed twice goes back in the deck and will turn up again. There are 67 cards. The Everything pack deals from all of them; each theme pack deals only cards from its theme.

Shiny cards. Every card can turn up in three finishes: Standard (about 7 in 10), Shiny (about 1 in 4) and Rainbow (about 1 in 20). Placing a card right on the first try moves it up one finish. Finishes are only for fun. No event is "rarer" than another.

Nothing to buy. Packs are free and never run out. There are no coins, timers or daily limits.

Joke items. About 1 pack in 12 has a silly extra, like a stick of old gum or a card from the "wrong pack." These are made up, and the junk drawer says so.

Saving. The binder is saved in this browser on this device. There is no account. Clearing the browser's data erases it.
